02 / ENTREPRENEURSHIP✿
bakeryLOVE♡
COCINARTE · PAGE PREVIEW

CocinArte | The joy of creating through flavor.

CocinArte was created as a school initiative for second-year high school students, so they could see what we were making, discover our baking activities, and learn about the products.

Our goal: help students understand ingredient costs, baking materials, and the recipes prepared in class.

Our approach: make baking more approachable by sharing activities, practical information, and the joy of creating something from scratch.

Clear prices: the project page shares product prices so students can review the cost and feel more confident before buying.
